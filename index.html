About me

I’m in my second year at Dronacharya College of Engineering (2024–2028), studying Computer Science and Engineering. I like the full path from raw data to a working app: cleaning, modelling, then building the interface.

This summer I completed the Data Science & AI track at IIT Jammu’s Summer School (10 June – 10 August 2026), run with I3C – IIT Jammu and Techible. It was project-based training in Python, data analysis, machine learning and visualization.

I practised preprocessing, EDA, feature encoding, model training, evaluation, cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning, and built three extra mini-projects: House Price Prediction, Employee Attrition Prediction and Heart Disease Prediction.
